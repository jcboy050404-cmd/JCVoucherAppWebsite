What is Voucher App?

Voucher App is a powerful mobile and desktop application designed specifically for MikroTik network administrators and hotspot owners. Whether you are managing a small café network, a hotel hotspot, or a large-scale community ISP, Voucher App provides all the essential tools to manage users, generate vouchers, and track sales seamlessly.

Say goodbye to complex Winbox configurations for everyday tasks. We bring the full power of RouterOS hotspot management into a clean, modern, and easy-to-use interface, letting you focus on growing your business while we handle the technical heavy lifting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I monitor and manage active accounts?

Yes, absolutely! The app allows you to view live active Hotspot users, PPPoE clients, and IPoE subscribers. You can monitor their connection times, bandwidth usage, and even manually disconnect them if needed directly from your phone.

Do I need to be on the same local network?

No, if your MikroTik router has a public IP address or if you are using a cloud tunnel service, you can connect to and manage your router remotely from anywhere in the world.

Is there a limit to how many vouchers I can generate?

You can effortlessly batch-generate hundreds of vouchers at once. Simply select your desired profile, set the data or time limits, and the app will generate the batch instantly.
